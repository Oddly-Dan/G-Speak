⚙️ Voice Settings

Off by default. When on, active moods subtly adjust speaking speed and pitch — e.g. Hungry/Sad slow and lower it a touch, Full/Happy speed it up and lift it. Multiple active moods stack.

Backup

Settings, moods, and your most-used words all live only on this device. Export them to keep a backup or move to another device.

Boards

Press and hold a chip or a board button to pin, unpin, remove, or add words. If it gets messy, this puts every category board (and the suggestion strip's pinned words) back to how it started. Your voice settings, moods, and usage history aren't touched.
